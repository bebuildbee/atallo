Rule 01

Authenticity Comes First

Audiences respond best to genuine experiences. Demonstrate how Allo Bank PayLater fits naturally into your own lifestyle instead of creating content that feels scripted or overly promotional.

  • Show Real Experiences — Share authentic moments instead of staged product demonstrations.
  • Explain the Benefits — Focus on how PayLater helps users, not simply what it offers.
  • Be Honest — Present information accurately and avoid exaggerated promises.
